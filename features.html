Features

Everything OmniTree can do, in short. Each part is a box you can switch on or off.

News

  • Add any news site that has a feed; a set of well-known international sources is ready to pick from.
  • The same story from several outlets is grouped into one line, with a count of how many reported it. Stories many outlets carry are marked as hot.
  • Grouping also works between languages, for example the same event in Swedish and English.
  • Choose which languages you want to see, filter by source, and hide items you do not want.
  • Search that goes further than the list: every loaded headline at once, then the last 30 days on your own news sites.
  • Reader: click a headline and scroll through all of them in one window, each with its picture and the full text of the article where the site gives it.
  • New headlines are marked until you have seen them.

Sports

  • Football from more than twenty countries (Champions League, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Allsvenskan, the Nordic leagues, MLS and more) with the big cups, the World Cup and the European Championship; NBA, NHL, SHL, NFL, MLB, college sports, rugby, cricket, Australian football, UFC, Formula 1, IndyCar, NASCAR, tennis, golf, and esports: Counter-Strike 2, League of Legends and Dota 2.
  • Live scores that update by themselves, upcoming games, and a view for your favorite sport.
  • Results: go back through finished games, fight cards, races and tournaments day by day, with the same full details as a live game.
  • Search the box for a team, player, fighter, league or event, across results, live games and fixtures.
  • Click a game for the details: goals and cards, team stats, the best players, line-ups, recent form, the league table, and odds before the game.
  • UFC: every fight of the card, fighter records and details, and odds.
  • Formula 1: the order of every session, plus driver and team standings. Tennis: the world ranking. Golf: the full leaderboard.
  • Esports: maps and scoreboards, team pages with roster and results, player pages, and a button to watch the match on Twitch inside the page.
  • Star your teams, fighters and players to get a notification when they play, score and finish, and to see their games in your calendar.
  • Click any team or player for their own page: league place, form, results and coming games, the squad, and each player's details and statistics. SHL teams add the club's history, team figures and top scorers. Tennis matches as clear scoreboards with every set.
  • Put any game, fight card, race or tournament in your calendar with a reminder in one click, or every coming event of a league at once, also the ones announced later.

TV shows

  • Follow the series you watch and see the next episode of each, day by day, and the ones that just aired.
  • “On today”: the best-known shows airing today in your country, on television and on streaming services.
  • Click a show for what it is about, its coming and latest episodes, rating and where it runs.
  • Episodes show in your calendar and in the summary, and you can be told the moment a new one airs.

Stocks and crypto

  • Your own list of stocks, indexes, currencies and crypto, with price and change.
  • A small chart on every line for today, the week or the month.
  • Add a stock by typing the company name and picking it from a list; no need to know the symbol.
  • Bring a whole portfolio in one go: a CSV file from your broker, a Google Sheet, a pasted list or a TradingView watchlist. Files with only ISIN codes work too.
  • Click a line for its chart and recent news about it: a clean price line where you can point anywhere for the price, from one day back to all time, or the full chart with candles and volume. The page remembers which you prefer.
  • Market hours at a glance: which of the exchanges your stocks trade on are open, and how long until each closes or opens, in your own time.
  • Sort by biggest mover, and get a notification when something moves more than a level you set.
  • Price alerts: “tell me when this stock is more than, less than or reaches a price”, set in one sentence and changed or removed just as easily.

Twitch

  • Shows which of the channels you follow are live, with game, title, viewers and how long they have been on.
  • Watch inside the page with chat, dock a stream at the side while you read, or pop it out.
  • Star streamers to be told when they go live.
  • Discover: the biggest live streams, any category ten at a time, and a search for any channel or game, for when nobody you follow is live. Put a channel on your own list without following it.

YouTube

  • The newest videos from the channels you add, without the recommendations.
  • Play inside the page or dock the player at the side.
  • Discover: the most viewed videos of today and this week, what is live, topics like music, gaming, news and sports, and a search for any video or channel. Open any channel's newest videos, and put a channel in your box with one click.

Reddit

  • Posts from the subreddits you choose, sorted hot, new or top.
  • Click a post to read it with the top comments, without leaving the page, and scroll on to the next posts in the same window.
  • Search your subreddits up to a year back.

Telegram

  • Follow public Telegram channels without the app or an account.
  • Photos and links are shown, older posts load on request, and new posts are marked.
  • Catch up in one go: a reader that opens at the oldest post you have not seen and lets you scroll through them one after another, on a phone too.
  • Search the whole history of your channels.

Mail

  • Your Gmail unread count on the page, for one or several inboxes, with sender and subject of the latest.
  • Set up with a small script in your own Google account, so the page never gets your password.

Weather

  • Now, hour by hour and the coming days for your city, with warnings for strong wind, heavy rain, snow, frost and heat.
  • Starts with the main city of your device's time zone, without asking for your position. Change it to your own city at any time.
  • Save several cities and switch between them.

Radio

  • More than 70 stations sorted by genre and language: pop, rock, hip-hop, electronic, reggae, jazz, classical, news and more.
  • Shows the song that is playing with cover, album and year, and the songs played before it.
  • Add your own stations: search a public catalogue of tens of thousands of stations by name, city or genre, listen first, then add.

Podcasts

  • The newest episodes of the podcasts you follow, in one list, with the new ones marked.
  • The most popular shows of your country, by topic, and a search of every podcast by name, topic or person.
  • Each show has its own page with its latest episodes, going further back on request.
  • Episodes play in the Radio box, which switches between radio and podcasts: jump back and forward, speed, volume, and a player with the notes of the episode and what comes next.
  • Where you stopped is remembered on every device. On a phone it keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Get a notification when a new episode of a show you starred comes out.

Notes and to-dos

  • Quick notes and a to-do list, pinned items, and paste-to-note. Change any note or task in place.
  • Add a note or a task straight from the search bar.
  • Read later: put any headline, post or video aside with one click and find it in its own list, on every device.

Calendar

  • Your own events, repeating events and birthdays with the age shown. Change any event later, and download them all as an .ics file for another calendar.
  • Public holidays for 15 countries, big and small days of the year, week numbers, full moons and your payday.
  • Bring in other calendars: import an .ics file, or follow a Google, Outlook or Apple calendar by its address so its events show next to yours and stay up to date.
  • Reminders, and a small line under the date that tells you what is next.

Date, time and world clocks

  • Choose what the top of the page shows (day, date, year, time, location) and whether it is written the European or the American way, with a 12- or 24-hour clock.
  • World clocks for up to five cities, in a small box when you point at the date or on the page under it.

Timers, stopwatch and calculator

  • Several at once, with names, sound and a notification when they finish. They keep running if you close the tab.
  • A calculator with brackets, powers, roots, trigonometry and logarithms, which you can also use from the keyboard and keep docked at the side.

Search bar

  • Search the web with the engine you prefer, or one site directly with short codes such as !yt and !w.
  • A calculator, unit and currency converter, date maths and a translator, answered right in the bar.
  • Search the whole page at once: start with ss, or press Shift+Enter, and every box is searched, from news and sports to your notes, calendar, bookmarks and mail.
  • Choose your own command words and keys.
  • Type help to see everything it can do.

Layout and looks

  • A bookmarks bar for your own links at the top of the page, with folders, icons and colors. Import the bookmarks of your browser from its export file and pick the folders you want.
  • Web page boxes: show up to three web pages of your choice in boxes of their own, such as a dashboard, a live map or a webcam, with automatic reload if you want it.
  • Edit the layout live: drag a box by its tab and see exactly where it lands, drag its edge or corner to resize it on a 12-column grid, with Undo and Cancel.
  • A phone keeps its own layout and text size: shorter lists, bigger text or another order on the phone leave the computer as it is.
  • Dock notes, the calendar, the calculator or a player at the side of the page. On a computer, drag a docked panel up, down, or to the other side, attach it right next to the boxes, and give it its own height and text size; the page stays centered while there is room.
  • Light and dark theme, your own accent color, text size, colors per box, rounded or square corners everywhere, and a wallpaper of your own.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for everything, which you can change.
  • Every setting is easy to find: Settings has a search field that finds any option by name, also the ones folded away under “More”.
  • Works on a phone, where the boxes stack in one column.

Notifications

  • Choose what you are told about: breaking stories, headlines with words you watch for, streamers going live, goals and results of your teams, new episodes of your podcasts, new mail, price moves and calendar reminders. On the page, as desktop notifications, or by email.
  • A bell keeps the latest ones.
  • Keeps updating while the tab is in the background, more calmly the longer you are away. When you come back, anything posted in between is filled in, so there is no need to reload the page.
  • A count in the browser tab, like (5) OmniTree, so you see from other tabs that something new is waiting. You choose what counts.
  • A summary of what happened while you were away: top stories, sports results, streams you missed, stock movers, mail and more. You choose the parts, their order, the sources and sports each one uses, how it looks, and whether it opens on your first visit of the day or whenever you come back after a while.
  • What you have seen, opened and cleared follows your account, so your phone and your computer agree and nothing is announced twice.

Getting started

  • A short setup guide asks your country and what you are into (news, football, other sports, investing, gaming, tech, music, podcasts), and starts your page with good sources for it: national and international news, active subreddits, official news channels on Telegram, your football league and the most popular podcasts of your country.
  • You choose the boxes yourself, and every source can be changed later.
  • Try it first: without an account the page is a working preview with sample content, real sports, TV shows and podcasts, and a short welcome that shows you around. Arrange it with Edit, then create an account and your layout comes with you.
  • Add another interest at any time, without losing anything you set up.

Account and safety

  • A free account saves everything and syncs it between devices within a minute; changes made on two devices are merged, not overwritten. Without an account, the page still works in your browser.
  • Download a backup of your whole setup and restore it later.
  • Two accounts never mix: when someone else signs in on the same browser, the previous account’s things are cleared from it first.
  • See every device that is signed in, sign the others out, and delete your account with everything in it at any time.
  • What is stored and why is written out in the privacy policy.
