OmniTree: your start page, everything in one tab

OmniTree is a free, customizable start page and personal dashboard for your browser. It puts the things you check every day side by side: news from the sources you choose, live sports scores and tables, stocks and crypto, the TV shows you follow, Twitch and YouTube, Reddit and Telegram, your unread mail, the weather, radio and podcasts, notes, to-dos, a calendar and timers. Scores, prices and headlines update by themselves while the tab is open.

You decide what is on the page: switch boxes on and off, drag them where you want them, and pick colors and a wallpaper. A free account saves your setup and brings it to every device.

Show any web page inside a box of its own, for example a dashboard, a live map, a webcam or a shared document. Paste its address and the box appears on your page; move and size it with Edit like any other box (H− and H+ change its height). All three are off until you fill one in.
Good to know: many large sites (Google, Facebook, banks, most news sites) do not allow being shown inside another page, and then the box stays empty. Sites that offer an “embed” address do work. The page in the box is that site’s own: it can load its own content and set its own cookies, but it cannot read anything from OmniTree.
