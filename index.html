OmniTree: your start page, everything in one tab

OmniTree is a free, customizable start page and personal dashboard for your browser. It puts the things you check every day side by side: news from the sources you choose, live sports scores and tables, stocks and crypto, Twitch and YouTube, Reddit and Telegram, your unread mail, the weather, radio, notes, to-dos, a calendar and timers. Scores, prices and headlines update by themselves while the tab is open.

You decide what is on the page: switch boxes on and off, drag them where you want them, and pick colors and a wallpaper. A free account saves your setup and brings it to every device.
